# Expenses Policy

This policy covers reimbursement of business expenses for all employees.

Expense claims must be submitted within 30 days of the date the expense was incurred.
Receipts are required for every claim above 25 pounds; card statements alone are not sufficient evidence.
Alcohol must never be claimed as a business expense, including when entertaining clients.
Rail travel must be booked in standard class unless the journey exceeds four hours.
Mileage may only be claimed for journeys in a private vehicle at the HMRC approved rate.
Hotel bookings must be made through the approved travel portal and must not exceed 150 pounds per night in the UK.
Claims on behalf of another employee must never be submitted; each person claims their own expenses.
Any expense above 500 pounds must be approved in writing by a director before it is incurred.
