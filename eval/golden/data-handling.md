# Data Handling Policy

This policy applies to all staff who handle customer or employee data.

Staff must lock their screens whenever they leave their desk, even for short breaks.
Customer data must never be copied to personal devices or personal cloud storage.
Paper records containing personal data must be shredded using the office confidential waste bins, not general waste.
Access to the customer database must only be requested through the IT service desk with a manager's written approval.
Any suspected data breach must be reported to the Data Protection Officer within 24 hours of discovery.
Personal data must not be sent by email to external recipients unless it is encrypted with the approved tool.
Staff must complete data protection refresher training every twelve months.
When working remotely, staff must use the company VPN before accessing any internal system that stores personal data.
