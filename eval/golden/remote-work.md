# Remote Working Policy

This policy sets the conditions for working away from the office.

Staff must ensure their home wifi router uses WPA2 or stronger encryption before connecting work devices.
Confidential calls must never be taken in public spaces such as cafes, trains, or shared workspaces.
The company VPN must be enabled before accessing any internal system from outside the office network.
Work devices must be locked or shut down when left unattended at home, including from family members.
Company documents must not be printed at home unless a manager has approved it in writing.
Staff must be reachable on the agreed collaboration tools during their contracted core hours, 10am to 4pm.
Any home-working equipment fault that affects security, such as a broken screen lock, must be reported to IT within one working day.
Working from outside the UK for more than 10 working days in a year requires prior written HR approval.
