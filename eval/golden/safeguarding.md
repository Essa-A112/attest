# Safeguarding Policy

This policy applies to all staff and volunteers who come into contact with children or vulnerable adults.

Concerns about a child's welfare must be reported to the Designated Safeguarding Lead within one working day.
Staff must never promise confidentiality to a child who discloses harm; explain that the concern must be shared.
Staff must never be alone with a child in a room with the door closed; use rooms with glass panels or keep doors open.
Physical contact must be limited to what is necessary, appropriate, and in view of other adults.
Personal contact details, including social media accounts, must never be shared with children or their families.
Photographs of children must only be taken on organisation devices with written parental consent on file.
Allegations against a member of staff must be reported directly to the Local Authority Designated Officer, bypassing line management if necessary.
All staff must complete safeguarding training before their first day of unsupervised contact.
